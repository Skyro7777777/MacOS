**TikTok’s Horizontal Full Screen (Landscape) Feature**

TikTok supports native **horizontal (landscape / 16:9) videos**. When a qualifying video appears in the For You or Following feed, a **“Full Screen”** button appears. Tapping it expands the video to true landscape orientation that fills the phone’s screen (users rotate the device). This is distinct from the normal vertical (9:16) feed experience.

### UI Details (Viewer Side)

**In the normal vertical feed:**
- Horizontal videos appear letterboxed (black bars top/bottom) so they fit the vertical layout.
- A clear **“Full Screen”** button/label is shown on or just below the video.
- The rest of the standard TikTok UI (username, caption, like/comment/share/favorite buttons on the right, progress bar at bottom, etc.) remains visible.

**After tapping Full Screen + rotating the phone:**
- Video expands to fill the entire screen in landscape (true 16:9, no black bars).
- UI becomes more immersive and cinema-like.
- Standard interactions stay available: Like, Comment, Favorite, Share, Follow.
- Playback controls include: progress scrubber, playback speed, and captions/subtitles.
- Scrolling (up/down) continues to work and switches the feed to mostly/other horizontal videos only (a “horizontal feed” mode). Sideways (left/right) swipes do not navigate.
- Exiting usually involves the back gesture/button or rotating back to portrait.

The feature also works well on TikTok TV (big-screen optimization) and larger devices (tablets/foldables), where landscape orientation and clearer video feed are better supported.

### Technical / Eligibility Requirements

**For the Full Screen button to appear:**
- Video must be **true landscape** (width > height, typically 16:9 aspect ratio).
- No text, stickers, or elements placed in the margins/letterbox areas (text must stay strictly inside the video frame).
- Preferred length: longer than 1 minute (up to the platform limit, commonly 10 minutes for these videos).
- Original content that follows Community Guidelines.
- Not lip-sync/random recordings, pure ads, or content from government/politician/political party accounts (in some reported criteria).

**How creators enable it:**
- Shoot or export the video already in landscape (do not force-rotate or heavily pad a vertical video).
- Upload the 16:9 file directly.
- Keep any on-screen text/captions inside the video boundaries (adding text outside the frame can disable the Full Screen option and force vertical treatment).
- TikTok detects the aspect ratio and automatically adds the Full Screen button for eligible videos.

Horizontal videos can receive algorithmic visibility boosts in some cases (especially longer ones). The feature started as a limited test around late 2022 and has since rolled out more widely, aligning TikTok closer to YouTube-style long-form viewing while keeping the core vertical feed intact.

**Note:** Availability can still vary slightly by account age, region, or whether the video is viewed from the main feed vs. profile/search/comments. The button is most reliable when the video is discovered organically in the For You/Following feed.
